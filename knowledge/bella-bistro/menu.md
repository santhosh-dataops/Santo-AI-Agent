# Hours and Location
Open Tuesday to Sunday, 5pm to 10pm. Closed Mondays. Weekend brunch Saturday and Sunday 10am to 2pm.
We are at 120 Main Street. Free parking behind the building.

# Menu
Margherita pizza $16. Truffle mushroom risotto $24. Spaghetti carbonara $21. Tiramisu $9.

# Dietary and Allergens
Vegan options: marinara pizza without cheese, grilled vegetable plate, and sorbet.
Gluten-free pasta is available for any pasta dish for $3 extra. Our kitchen handles nuts.

# Reservations
We take reservations for parties up to 10. Larger groups and private events: events@bella.example.
