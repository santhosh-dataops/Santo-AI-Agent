# PTO Policy
Full-time employees receive 20 PTO days per year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days roll over.
Request PTO in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for absences over 3 days.

# Payroll
Payday is every other Friday. Pay stubs are available in the HR portal.

# Benefits
Medical, dental and vision coverage starts on your first day. The company matches 401k contributions
100% up to 4% of salary, vesting immediately.

# Onboarding
New hires complete I-9 verification in week one and get laptop setup from IT on day one.
