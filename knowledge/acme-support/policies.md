# Shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders over $50 and takes 3-5 business days within the US.
Express shipping costs $15 and arrives in 1-2 business days. We ship to the US and Canada only.
Orders placed before 2pm CT ship the same day.

# Returns
You can return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Start a return at acme.example/returns. Return shipping is free for exchanges and $6.95 for refunds.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive the item.

# Warranty
Tents, backpacks and sleeping bags have a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover wear and tear, misuse or accidental damage.
To file a claim, email photos and your order number to warranty@acme.example.
