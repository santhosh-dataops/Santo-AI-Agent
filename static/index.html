Frequently asked questions

Do I need any technical skills?

No. You fill in a simple form, paste your information and copy one line onto your website. If you use Shopify, WordPress or Wix, it takes a couple of minutes.

Can Santo answer any question?

Yes. Santo answers questions about your business from your own information, and general questions too: facts, explanations, math, dates and everyday advice. It never makes up your prices or policies. If something about your business isn't covered, it offers to pass the customer's details to your team.

Can it book appointments or tables?

Yes. Santo collects the customer's details and sends them to your booking tool, calendar or email, so nothing gets missed.

Can I change what it says later?

Any time. Update your information or instructions and Santo uses the new version straight away.

Do I need an OpenAI or other AI account?

No. Santo comes with its own open-source AI model that runs on your server, so there are no API keys and no per-message charges.

Is my information safe?

Your data is kept separate from other businesses and you can delete conversations at any time. Because the AI runs on your own server, customer conversations are never sent to an outside AI company.
